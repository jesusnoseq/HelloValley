- **Output and format**: the character set is its own folder, `packages/client/public/assets/characters/` (committed, about 0.6 MB optimized: `body.glb` 166 kB, most of it the 14 sampled clips; 16 `animal.<id>.glb` and 9 `outfit.<npc>.glb` of 8–25 kB each), with `character-manifest.json` (zod: `CharacterManifestSchema`). - **worldTime semantics** (my choice; M16 owns the clock): `worldTime` is the phase of the 40-minute day, linear in real time as in architecture §3.1. ## M10

- **Split**: recipes, their zod schema and the assembler math live in `packages/shared/src/buildings/` (headless: `assembleBuilding(recipe, kitManifest)` returns module placements with position, quarter turns and scale, door sockets and bounds). The client side is `scene/kitLoader.ts` (style-aware, cached kit loader), `scene/buildingMeshes.ts` (merges every placed variant into one geometry per material slot), `scene/KitBuilding.tsx`, `scene/SignIcons.tsx` and `scene/KitLighting.tsx`. Architecture §4 lists the assembler under `client/scene/`; only the pure placement math moved to shared, so M11 (doors, walkability) can use it without three.js.
- **Recipe format** (GDD §7.2 gives only a sketch): building frame = module frame (pivot at the front-left footprint corner, front facade on z = 0 facing +Z). Every footprint corner cell gets `corner.outer` (turned per corner), and facade rows list only the cells between the corners (w − 2 front/back, d − 2 left/right), one row per floor, the last row repeating upwards. This differs from the GDD sketch, where a 3-wide footprint lists 3 front modules: `corner.outer` is a full 1 m L of plain wall, so corner cells cannot hold a window or door. Consequence: buildings are at least 3 cells wide and rectangular (no L-shapes, so `corner.inner` is unused for now). Attachments name `side`/`floor`/`slot`; props are free-standing style-kit pieces (`bench`, `lamp`, `fence`, `stair`) with a quarter-turn `rot` and a `count` for fence runs. Validation also checks attachment `fits:` tags (mirrored in `ATTACHMENT_FITS`, a test compares it with the manifest), a front door on the ground floor, balconies in front of every upper-floor door, no shopfronts above ground, no dormers on hip roofs, no sign under an awning and no props inside the footprint.
- **Recipe ids** are the place ids from `PLACE_IDS` (`farm` is the farm barn). Recipes use only style-kit modules; town decoration from the common kit (crates, flowers, market stall) is left to M11.
- **Variants**: one `variant` per building for all walls and corners (roofs, attachments and props can override). To make that coherent, the europe generator now gives every wall-like module the same plaster swatch per variant index (`WALL_SWATCH_BY_VARIANT` = sage, yellow, cream; `wall.timber` keeps its own infill). Before, `wall.window` v1 could be yellow next to a pink `wall.door` v1. Only `wall.door`, `wall.shopfront`, `corner.outer` and `corner.inner` GLBs changed (rebuilt with Blender 5.2.1, manifest unchanged); a Python test checks the rule. Kits with fewer variants wrap the index.
- **Roof scaling** (the M05 TODO): the 2 × 2 roof is scaled along the ridge by the footprint and across it with height and depth scaled together, so the pitch is unchanged. Eave and gable overhangs and the slab thickness scale too (on the 7 × 4 apartment block the eaves overhang 0.5 m instead of 0.25 m). Stretched hip roofs become stretched pyramids rather than gaining a ridge. A 9-slice deformation that keeps the overhangs fixed would look better; default: plain scaling, as the M05 note planned. Chimneys and dormers are not scaled; they mate with the scaled roof's `chimney`/`dormer` sockets (dormers spread along the ridge on the front slope; with `ridge: "z"` that slope faces +X).
- **Rendering**: one merged mesh per material slot per building (atlas, glass, glow), about 3 draw calls plus the shadow pass, as GDD §7.2 says ("merged by material per building"). Geometry is de-quantized to Float32 (meshopt-quantized attributes cannot take a transform), which costs memory; instancing across buildings was not done. The atlas is bound at load (`flipY = false`, sRGB) on one shared material per kit and slot. KTX2 atlases throw a clear error: there is no KTX2 output yet (no `toktx`), so the Basis transcoder is TODO(M37).
- **Night**: `KitLighting` drives lamp `glow` (0.2 + 6 × nightGlow, like the M09 placeholders) and a warm window emission on `glass` (0.8 × nightGlow, below the bloom threshold) for every loaded kit. This covers the M09 "window glow on real buildings" TODO.
- **Signs**: `sign.hanging` takes an `icon` (`SIGN_ICONS`); the client paints a placeholder glyph (emoji on a canvas texture) on both board faces at the `icon` sockets. The hand-drawn icon set is TODO(M37). Difficulty leaves on signs are M11.
- **Style switching hooks**: `dioramaStore.style` + `setStyle`, `AVAILABLE_STYLE_KITS` (only `european`) and `resolveKitStyle` (other styles fall back to the European kit until M32–M34). `KitBuilding` keeps the old meshes until the new kit's are built, then swaps them in place; a test checks that placements and doors are identical across styles. Setting the style from the player profile/Settings is TODO(M14/M37).
- **Ground height**: buildings stand at y = 0 while M06 grass tops out at 0.1 m. Default: M11 decides (sink the tiles under buildings or lift buildings by 0.1 m).
- **Dev page**: `/dev/buildings` (dev server only) shows all 10 buildings (2 rows of 5 along the screen horizontal), a style picker, a single-building view and the camera/time controls; query parameters `?b=bakery&style=european&t=0.85&speed=0&zoom=1&step=0&fx=0`. `DioramaView`/`DioramaCanvas` gained `world` and `shadowExtent` props.
