# Asset credits

Where each shipped asset comes from, and its licence. Add an entry whenever a clip, model,
texture or font comes from somewhere new.

## Audio (build plan M35)

Every clip in `packages/client/public/assets/audio/` is a **generated placeholder**. No sound was
downloaded, sampled or recorded. `tools/audio/generate-audio.ts` (`npm run assets:audio`) builds
each one from oscillators, filtered noise and Karplus–Strong plucks, with a fixed seed per clip,
and encodes it to mono Ogg/Opus with the local ffmpeg (libopus). The clips are original works of
this project and carry no third-party licence. They are about 1.5 MB in total, inside the 3 MB
`AUDIO_BUDGET_BYTES` that a client test checks.

The list of clips is the catalog in `packages/shared/src/audio/catalog.ts`.

| Clips | Files | What they are |
|---|---|---|
| Music, European | `music-european-day.ogg`, `music-european-night.ogg` | A musette waltz (synthesised accordion, plucked guitar, shaker), and a slow minor ballad (guitar arpeggios, soft accordion pad) |
| Music, Mediterranean | `music-mediterranean-{day,night}.ogg` | A mixolydian tune on tremolo mandolin over strummed guitar, and the Andalusian cadence on plucked guitar |
| Music, Arabic | `music-arabic-{day,night}.ogg` | Maqam Hijaz: oud melody over a drone with a maqsum frame-drum rhythm, and qanun arpeggios |
| Music, Japanese | `music-japanese-{day,night}.ogg` | Koto in the yo scale with shakuhachi, and the in scale with breathy shakuhachi and sparse koto |
| Ambience | `amb-river.ogg`, `amb-birds.ogg`, `amb-crickets.ogg`, `amb-chatter.ogg`, `amb-rain.ogg` | Seamless loops: a river wash with babble and bubbles, bird calls, cricket trills, a distant crowd murmur (buzz through vowel formants), and rain hiss with droplets |
| Footsteps | `step-{grass,dirt,cobble,sand,wood,stone,tile}-{0,1,2}.ogg` | Three variations per surface, built from thumps, knocks and filtered noise |
| Effects | `ui-pop.ogg`, `ui-open.ogg`, `ui-close.ogg`, `door-chime.ogg`, `coins.ogg`, `fish-cast.ogg`, `fish-splash.ogg`, `fish-reel.ogg`, `cook-sizzle.ogg`, `pickup.ogg`, `page.ogg` | UI pops and plucks, a two-note door bell, coin clinks, the fishing whoosh, splash and reel ratchet, a pan sizzle, a pick-up arpeggio and a page swish |

These placeholders are meant to be replaced by produced music and recorded effects before
launch. A replacement keeps the catalog's file name, and a loop keeps the catalog's
`loopSeconds` (the player loops exactly that span). A royalty-free replacement must be listed
here with its source, author and licence.

## Other assets

- Style kits, the common and interior kits, and the characters (`public/assets/kits/`,
  `public/assets/characters/`) are generated by this project's Blender scripts in
  `tools/blender/` (M04–M08, M32–M34). They contain no third-party models or textures.
- UI and script fonts (Noto families) are loaded at run time from Google Fonts
  (`fonts.googleapis.com`, SIL Open Font License). They are not committed to the repository.
- NPC voices are generated at play time by Gemini TTS through Firebase AI Logic (M22). They are
  not committed to the repository.
